Lapse, renewal & out-of-sequence processing

Special handling rules that keep the lifecycle consistent

⏱️ Lapse functionality (Renewal & Reinstatement)

If a renewal offer is not accepted, or premium is not received, by the term expiration date, the policy moves to a Lapsed state rather than immediately cancelling — preserving continuity for a defined grace window.

  1. Renewal generated N days before expiration and monitored for acceptance/payment.
  2. No response by expiration → status set to Lapsed; coverage suspended, grace period starts.
  3. Reinstatement request within grace window re-validates eligibility, risk changes and outstanding balance.
  4. Approved reinstatement restores continuous coverage; effective-dated back to lapse date or resumes prospectively per rule.
  5. Beyond grace window, lapsed policy is closed and requires new business submission.

🔀 Out-of-sequence (OOS) transaction processing

Occurs when a transaction is entered with an effective date earlier than another transaction already processed on the policy (e.g. a backdated endorsement after a renewal has already run).

  1. System detects the effective-date conflict against the transaction history / financial ledger.
  2. Policy is flagged for OOS review; automatic premium and document recalculation is attempted.
  3. Downstream transactions (renewal, billing, endorsements) are re-sequenced and re-rated as needed.
  4. Admin/underwriter reviews recalculated deltas, approves financial adjustments and reissues affected documents.
  5. Audit trail captures original sequence, conflict, and resolution for full traceability.

Allocation rules, suspense & delinquency handling

How payments are matched, exceptions resolved and non-pay signals reach Policy

📐 Payment allocation rules

Incoming payments are matched and applied automatically using a configurable priority order, with manual override available to CSR/Admin personas.

  1. Match payment to invoice/instalment using policy, account or remittance reference.
  2. Apply in priority order: fees & taxes → oldest earned premium → current instalment → future instalment.
  3. Unmatched or partial-reference payments are routed to Suspense pending identification.
  4. Residual small-balance amounts beyond tolerance threshold are routed for Write-off approval.
  5. Credit balances trigger overpayment review and, once approved, a Disbursement refund.

📉 Delinquency, payment status & non-pay cancellation

Billing continuously publishes account status back to Policy Administration so coverage decisions stay in sync with payment reality.

  1. Missed instalment ages the account into Past Due, then Delinquent per configurable grace days.
  2. Payment status (Current / Past Due / Delinquent / Written-off) is synced to Policy on every change.
  3. Dunning notices generated and dispatched at configured delinquency milestones.
  4. Unresolved delinquency triggers a non-payment cancellation indicator sent to Policy Administration.
  5. Payment received before the cancellation effective date clears the indicator and halts cancellation.

Document Management System & external integration

How generated forms are governed, retrieved and shared outside the platform

🗄️ Document Management System (DMS) considerations

  1. Every generated document is indexed with policy/account, form type, effective date and persona/actor metadata.
  2. Version history is preserved for reissued or corrected documents, with full lineage back to the original template.
  3. Retention and disposition rules apply per document type and regulatory requirement.
  4. Documents are searchable and retrievable by CSR, underwriting, customer self-service and audit functions.
  5. Access is governed by role-based permissions and full retrieval activity is logged for audit.

🔗 External API / third-party integration

  1. Forms Engine exposes an external API call at processing time to pass form data/content to third-party systems.
  2. Used for e-signature providers, regulatory filing services, state reporting and partner document exchanges.
  3. Payload includes merged field data, document reference/ID and callback status endpoint.
  4. Third-party response (accepted / signed / rejected) is written back into the forms audit trail.
  5. Failures are retried with backoff and surfaced as alerts to the Admin persona.

Real-time data management

How Policy, Billing and Claims data is unified for reporting

⚡ Real-time collection & integration

  1. Policy, Billing and Claims each publish domain events / CDC streams as transactions occur.
  2. Events are correlated by policy, account, claim and party ID as they land in the data platform.
  3. Operational Data Store provides a near-real-time cross-domain view for operational reporting.
  4. Data Lake / Warehouse curates and historises the same data for trend, actuarial and regulatory reporting.

📈 Management-level reporting

  1. Semantic layer exposes subject-area models (policy, billing, claims, party) to BI & reporting tools.
  2. Persona-based dashboards apply role-based filters and access controls over the same governed data.
  3. Management dashboards blend all three modules — growth, loss ratio, delinquency and claims trends.
  4. All downstream reports remain point-in-time consistent and fully auditable back to source events.
